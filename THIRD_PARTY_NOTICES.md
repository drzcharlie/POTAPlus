## OpenHamClock

Project: https://github.com/accius/openhamclock

POTAPlus bundles P.533 WebAssembly loader/runtime files obtained from the OpenHamClock project and retrieves monthly P.533 coefficient data through OpenHamClock's data endpoint.

## Parks on the Air

POTAPlus retrieves park information and coordinates from the POTA API for the visible POTA references.

Parks on the Air and POTA are marks/names of their respective organization. POTAPlus is an independent helper extension and is not represented as an official POTA product.

## NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center

POTAPlus retrieves current public space-weather information from NOAA SWPC, including solar flux and geomagnetic/X-ray data, for use as propagation-model inputs/context.
